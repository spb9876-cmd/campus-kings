Who we are

01

The Belt is the prize

The Campus King Belt goes to whoever racks up the most national championships across the 20 accredited seasons. Rings matter most. That part doesn't change.

02

Playoff Points reward the climb

A parallel points race tracks the whole cycle — making the bracket, winning playoff games, reaching the Final Four, taking your conference. Being one of the five best coaches over 20 seasons should mean something too.

03

Nobody holds up the league

We advance on a 24-hour schedule at 8 PM ET. You need to be able to play between 7 PM and midnight ET. Games get scheduled in user chats, and admins rule on anything that stalls.

04

Games get streamed

User games get streamed. It keeps things transparent, keeps everybody honest, and gives the rest of the league something to watch on a Tuesday night.

How a week works

Advance

The league advances every 24 hours at 8 PM ET. Know whether you have a game.

Schedule

User chats get created for each matchup. Lead with your availability, be specific about time zones, tag your opponent.

Play

Heisman difficulty, 5-minute quarters, default settings. Stream your user games. CPU games are simmed unless you want to play them.

Report

Scores get logged, the site updates, and the recap and power rankings follow.
