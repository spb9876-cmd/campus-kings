Championship Week is here, one trophy has already changed hands, and the man who took it is the same one who's been living in the champ's head since Week 9. This is the real postseason preview — not projections, not seeding math. Four title games. One is final. Three decide everything else.

## SEC Championship — FINAL: Ole Miss 33, Oklahoma 26

**Pat beat Stew again.** Not a regular-season Saturday this time — a trophy game, and it makes him SEC Champion and the first coach in the accredited era to beat Stew twice in one season. Per the league broadcast, Heisman front-runner **Jeremy Payne** led the way, and the Rebels closed a one-score game against the best closer this league has ever had. The handshake in the game chat afterward was two letters — "Gg" — which is about as much as anybody's ever gotten out of Stew after a loss.

Before the Norman faithful panic: we've seen this exact movie. In Season One, Stew **lost the SEC Championship** — 52-49 to Work — and answered by winning the national title anyway as a low seed. Losing a conference final is, historically, the most dangerous thing you can do to this man. HOWEVA. Nobody had ever beaten him twice in a season before. Nobody had held him to 26 in a game he needed. Pat isn't chipping at the aura anymore; he's dismantling it on schedule.

## Big Ten Championship — Michigan (Dre) vs Oregon (Bowzer)

The rivalry-game survivor against the league's quietest unbeaten. Dre comes in off a four-overtime, 21-point-comeback epic in The Shoe with his perfect season intact; Bowzer has spent thirteen weeks winning by three scores and posting everybody ELSE's highlights in #big-games. Now he has to cover his own game. Two of the hottest coaches in the league, one banner, and the winner walks into the bracket as the B1G's standard-bearer.

---

## ACC Championship — Clemson (LT) vs Miami (Lute)

The rematch nobody in Miami wanted. LT beat Lute 31-10 in last season's ACC final on the way to his second straight national championship game, and Clemson closed this regular season rolling (35-17 over Florida State in Week 12). Lute's answer all year has been defense and patience. Somebody's conference-title drought — or streak — moves by one this week.

---

## Big 12 Championship — Arizona State (TBO) vs TCU (KJ)

An exact rerun of last season's Big 12 final, which TBO won 26-15 to cap the biggest rise the league had ever seen. KJ's been quieter this year but arrives off a 52-point outburst against Baylor. The two best new-program builds in league history, one trophy, and a seed at stake — last year the winner of this game walked in as the lowest-seeded conference champion and nobody wants that draw twice.

---

## What It All Means for the Belt

Stew is 2-for-2 on belts in the accredited era, and both times the road ran through games exactly like the ones this week produces. The difference in Season Three: for the first time, there's a coach with documented, repeated, trophy-stakes wins over him — and that coach just took the SEC crown out of Norman. The bracket seeds off these results. If Stew's three-peat happens now, it happens the hard way, from the wrong side of the seeding table, through a field that finally believes he's mortal.

And somewhere in the group chat, the man who owns the only belt this league has ever awarded is typing "Gg" and loading the film.
