## No. 1

**Stew — Oklahoma.** The last edition put an asterisk on the throne. Consider it removed. Beating Pat 38-20 is the exact result the résumé needed: his first win over the champ since Season Two, at stakes, by three scores. The Norman loss to JT still sits in the file — nothing on this board gets erased — but the question that edition asked ("what does Stew do about it?") now has an answer with a date on it. Two rings, and the best win of Season Four. The chair is his by every measure again.

## No. 2

**Pat — Ole Miss.** First loss of the defense, and it came to the one coach the board ranks above him — which is exactly what the board predicted and exactly what Pat can't afford in a bracket. If these two meet in the playoffs, it will be the biggest game in league history. The board is patient. So is he, presumably.

## No. 3

**Tayne — Notre Dame.** The only unbeaten contender left: 4-0 in played games, with wins over Jason, Work, Harry, and Quan. The gap between No. 2 and No. 3 has never been thinner. A Tayne-Pat playoff game would settle an argument this board has been having with itself for a month.
