The second board of Season Seven, all twenty-five chairs, measured against the Shoe Edition. Records are league games through Week 10, played and forfeited; the notes say which is which, because this stretch had fourteen forfeits in twenty-seven games and the board refuses to pretend otherwise.

## No. 1 — Stew, Oklahoma (7-0)

Seven played, seven won, including 29-14 over the champ and 37-30 over Work. The only coach in the league who has played every week. "Ole miss is dog shit," he wrote afterward, and the champ agreed. Four rings, 4-2, and an eleventh straight played win.

## No. 2 — Curt, Ohio State (4-0)

Three played wins — KJ, Bowzer, 49 on Teeric — and a Roy forfeit. The founder has not lost a game of any kind. Columbus is the other half of the Big Ten title game.

## No. 3 — Purp, LSU (5-0) ▲2

Unbeaten, but two of the last three were forfeits (Thrash, Bawce). The played résumé is 70 on Livid, 35 on Jordan, 28-7 on Silingi. The board would still like to see him against a top-five coach.

## No. 4 — Bowzer, Oregon (3-1) ▲4

Three straight wins, all played: Roy by four, **Dre by two at Autzen**, and 47 on the rookie at Washington. The only loss is the rain game in Columbus. The best three-week stretch anyone has put together, and it came through the vault.

## No. 5 — Lux, Clemson (4-0) ▼1

4-0, but the last two were forfeits and he has not played since The Rematch in Week 4. The board drops him one for the idle fortnight, not the record.

## No. 6 — Dre, Michigan (3-1)

Forfeit wins over Zoe and States, then a 17-15 loss in Eugene. One played win all year, in Week 1, over the champ. The vault's résumé is thinner than its record; the board holds him here on the Pat game and the two-point loss.

## No. 7 — LT, Notre Dame (2-0) ▲3

37-0 and 42-14, the second one a Game of the Week over Jarvis. Two played, 79-14 combined. If he plays a third, he's top five.

## No. 8 — Pat, Ole Miss (3-2) ▲1

One played win (62 on TBO), two forfeit wins (Jordan, Jason), two played losses (Dre, the king). "Indeed." The rings are the reason this is eighth and not twelfth.

## No. 9 — Bawce, Texas A&M (3-2) ▼6

Lost 31-7 to a rookie in Gainesville, then forfeited to LSU. The 3-0 start was real; so was Week 7.

## No. 10 — Work, Tennessee (2-2) ▲1

Forfeited to Jason, beat Quan 17-7, pushed the king to 37-30. The last line is the one the board graded.

## No. 11 — Jarvis, Miami (2-2) ▼4

42-14 to LT in the Catholics vs. Convicts GOTW. Two played wins (States, Zoe), two played losses (Stew, LT). Exactly what the schedule said he'd be.

## No. 12 — KJ, TCU (2-2) ▲2

42-9 over Thrash. The conference-only win streak continues; the losses outside it were to the two best coaches in the league.

## No. 13 — Jordan, Florida (2-3) ▲5

## No. 14 — TBO, Arizona State (2-2) ▼1

No league game between Weeks 6 and 10. The board can't move him up for games he didn't play.

## No. 15 — Lute, Texas (2-1) ▲10
