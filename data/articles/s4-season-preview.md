## The five questions of Season Four

### 1. What does Stew do about it?

The two-time champ got swept by Pat in the regular season, knocked out No. 1 Michigan from the 8-seed anyway, and then watched Bowzer erase him from fifteen down. His response window is open. Stew is still the winningest coach in league history by a mile — an 18-4 league mark with a 9-1 playoff record across the accredited era — and the CK Rankings kept him at No. 1 through the wreckage precisely because of seasons like the one he's about to have. Oklahoma with a grudge is the scariest team in the league until proven otherwise.

### 2. Can Pat repeat — and actually take the belt lead?

Nobody has beaten Pat in a game that mattered since midseason. A second straight title would tie the belt race at two and flip the entire twenty-season narrative on its head. The champ says he's going for broke. The recruiting board will tell us if that's a plan or a mood.

### 3. Who breaks through from the almost club?

LT has two title-game appearances and no ring. Dre went 13-0 and didn't survive the quarterfinals. Bowzer was one point from a championship. Purp, Lute, and Jason all made the bracket and all went home early. That is a lot of very good coaches with a very specific kind of scar tissue, and only one of them gets to fix it per season.

### 4. Do the new guys bite back?

JT gets his first full season at Nebraska — and immediately counted seven conference user games on his schedule. "Man… how I end up with 10 user games," he asked the chat, which answered the way this league answers everything. "lol you the goat…. Everyone want a piece," Bawce told him. Lux gets his first full season at Cal carrying four legacy rings from before the accredited cycle, and Fields Finest gets year two of the Auburn project. New blood usually pays tuition in this league. Usually.

### 5. Does the SEC hold the crown?

Stew called his shot on the conference, if not the coach: "One of them boys from the Sip got the next one." The SEC has produced two of the three accredited champions and half the playoff field every year. The B1G has Dre's machine and Bowzer's near-miss roster. The Big 12 has KJ's TCU and a TBO team playing angry. Somebody outside the SEC has to actually finish it.
