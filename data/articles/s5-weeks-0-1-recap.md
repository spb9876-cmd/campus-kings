Season Five's first advance hadn't even finished loading before the storyline wrote itself. Pat's Rebels — back-to-back champs, the unanimous No. 1 on the last CK Rankings board, a résumé the league had run out of qualifiers for — opened defense of the crown by getting run out of Oxford. **Penn State 38, Ole Miss 28.** States called it in #big-games before the site could: "🚨CHAMPS HUNGOVER🚨 Penn State gets the Big Ten on the board first as they roll into Oxford and humble the reigning back to back champs." The league's own recap bot put it plainer in the group chat a few hours later: "States walked into your building and left with your lunch money."

The belt is tied 2-2 with sixteen accredited seasons still to go, and one half of that tie just lost a game he was favored to win by every metric the board has. Nobody's writing him off after one week — the film that got him to No. 1 doesn't evaporate on a Tuesday — but Season Five now has a question mark on it that Season Four never did.

## Stew Opens 2-0, and Tayne Has Had Enough

The other half of the belt tie had a better Week Zero. **Oklahoma 26, Notre Dame 0** — the second meeting between these two on record, and worse for Tayne than the first: Stew's Sooners won 28-3 back in Season Three, and this time they didn't even let Notre Dame on the board. It set off exactly the reaction you'd expect in the main chat. Tayne, unprompted and at length: "Something's get old after awhile bro... I know I'm tired of it I've played you more than a lot of people in here... Check your record vs mines overall. Then count the rings." Stew's answer ran four words: "Idk what else to do." States — fresh off mugging the champ — watched from the sideline and summed up the room's mood: "How stew lining up the whole SEC."

He backed it up in Week One. **Oklahoma 35, Oregon 7** — Bowzer's Oregon, Season Four's Big Ten champ and a Final Four team, run over by 28. Two games into the season, Stew's outscored his opponents 61-7. If the board's own belt-race rule holds — the leader stays No. 1 until he's actually dethroned — Stew just spent Week One making the case against the man currently ranked ahead of him, who's 0-1.

## The Rest of Week One

- **Ohio State 33, Texas A&M 14** — Curt over Bawce. Bawce's own postgame, in the game's own channel, needed no embellishing: "Good game… too many turnovers."
- **Michigan 29, Georgia 10** — Dre over Iceman. Quiet, clean, exactly the kind of week one Dre's been running since Season Four's Final Four run.
- **Nebraska 28, Texas 24** — JT's second straight signature scalp to open a season. He beat Stew as a rookie in Norman last year; now he's beating a Texas team that made noise in Season Four. Work's reaction in the main chat needed no translation either: "Lost to a damn corner route fuckkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkk me."

## The Early Ledger

Six games into Season Five: Stew's 2-0 with a shutout and a blowout, the two-time champ is 0-1, and JT is off to the kind of start that got him Rookie of the Year buzz twelve months ago. The 24-hour advance keeps rolling, the belt race is still 2-2 at the top, and after one week, the coach the board just crowned No. 1 is the one with something to prove.
