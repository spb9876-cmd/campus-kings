## Jason Finds His Season Just in Time

Three weeks ago Alabama was 1-3 — a home loss to Miami, a five-point loss to Stew, a three-point loss to Purp. Then: **Alabama 37, South Carolina 13** in Week 10. **Alabama 49, LSU 7** in Week 11. **Alabama 35, Georgia 14** — the Week 12 Game of the Week — in Week 12. Three straight, 121 points scored, 34 allowed, and the last one came against a team that beat Florida by 21 the week before. Jason is 4-3 now instead of buried, and if the SEC bubble comes down to who's playing the best football right now, nobody else has an argument.

## Oklahoma Survives, Doesn't Cruise

**Oklahoma 27, Tennessee 21** was the Week 11 Game of the Week, and it wasn't the Stew of old — this one came down to the final possession against Purp's Vols. Two weeks after the worst loss of his tenure, the champ found a way to win ugly instead of losing pretty. That's still 5-1, still atop the SEC race on paper, and still very much alive. ## Penn State Isn't Playing Games

Literally. **Penn State 42, Indiana 20** in Week 10 and **Penn State 61, USC 14** in Week 11 — States outscored two overmatched Big Ten opponents 103-34 across the window. Penn State sits at 5-1, right in the thick of a Big Ten picture that's about to get very crowded.
