The first board of Season Seven, all twenty-five chairs. Movement is measured against the Glendale Edition, the final board of Season Six, so a coach who sat out the playoffs with a bad record can climb a long way on three good weeks. Records are league games only — played and forfeited — through Week 5.

## No. 1 — Stew, Oklahoma (3-0)

The Battle of the Champions, the Bro Bowl, and 50-7 on Alabama. Two of the three wins came against his own crew and neither was close enough to argue about. Fourth ring, 4-2 belt, twelve straight since the Buddy Bowl and counting. "go get my belt," he told KJ. Nobody has.

## No. 2 — Curt, Ohio State (2-0) ▲7

Two Games of the Week, two wins: the first victory of his career over KJ, 34-17, and then Oregon in a rainstorm, 31-27, with a 38-yard pick six for the cushion. "I have never beat you. This is just another game." He is 4-0 in-game and the only coach besides the king with two top-ten scalps.

## No. 3 — Bawce, Texas A&M (3-0) ▲12

Fifty on Zoe, 49 on Thrash, and a forfeit over Roy. Two played games, 99 points. The schedule complaint was fair and so is the ranking; the board will revisit it when the SEC schedule turns on him in Week 10.

## No. 4 — Lux, Clemson (2-0) ▲3

Shut out Jason, then won The Rematch in Tempe 31-29 without the play he spent a week mourning. "Every rule is a lux patch bro I suck." The board disagrees with the second clause.

## No. 5 — Purp, LSU (2-0) ▲1

Seventy on the rookie at Louisville and 35-10 on the rookie at Florida. Averaging 52. Death Valley has not met a contender yet; the board would like to see it.

## No. 6 — Dre, Michigan (1-0) ▼3

Beat the two-time champ 35-21 in Week 1. One game, one statement, no forfeits yet. The vault opens on time this year, so far.

## No. 7 — Jarvis, Miami (2-1) NEW

Lost the opener to the king, then beat States and Zoe. The original Jarvis is back with the original résumé — four legacy rings — and a 2-1 start that would be 3-0 against anybody but Stew.

## No. 8 — Bowzer, Oregon (0-1) ▼6

One league game, a four-point loss in the rain at the Shoe. The 13-0 runner-up season is a year old now and the board only pays for the current one. Still the most credible contender outside Norman; still 0-1.

## No. 9 — Pat, Ole Miss (1-1) ▼4

Lost to Dre in Week 1, scored 62 on TBO in Week 3. Two rings, the second-best point total in the league, and a Week 6 trip to Gainesville the desk has already named the SEC Battle.

## No. 10 — LT, Notre Dame (1-0) ▲11

A 37-0 debut over Roy in South Bend. One game is not a season, but it is the first league win of any kind on LT's ledger since the Clemson years.

## No. 11 — Work, Tennessee (1-0) NEW

Back after a season away and a Week 1 win over the rookie at Washington, 31-28. Texas history intact, new conference, same Work.

## No. 12 — States, Penn State (1-1) ▲1

Lost 31-21 to Jarvis — "fought hard," per Stew — then put 44 on USC. The board has seen this half of the calendar before.

## No. 13 — TBO, Arizona State (2-2) ▼2

Beat Roy and Silingi, gave up 62 to Pat, and lost The Rematch by two at home. The Cinderella engine is running but it is not pulling.

## No. 14 — KJ, TCU (1-2) ▼4

A forfeit win and two losses to the top two coaches on this board, by two and by seventeen. "i can't win a game outside my conference." The Big 12 still runs through Fort Worth; the board does not.

## No. 15 — Cobo, Washington (1-1) NEW

Lost by three to Work, beat Teeric by four. A rookie with a league win in Week 2 is ahead of every rookie on this board except the one in Norman seven years ago.

## No. 16 — Jason, Georgia (0-1) ▲6

Shut out by Lux in the Georgia debut. One game played is still one more than the last two seasons combined.

## No. 17 — Quan, Alabama (0-1) ▼3
