Championship week is done, the bracket is up, and Season Six has produced a sentence nobody wrote on their preseason card: **the two unbeaten teams are coached by Bowzer and a rookie.** The king is a 3-seed. The two-time champ is a 4. The first round starts tonight. Let's take it from the top.

## The stretch run (Weeks 11-14)

- **The Game: Michigan 27, Ohio State 13.** Dre over Curt, again, for the third straight season — the founder's only loss of the year, and it was the one that mattered to him most.
- **Oklahoma 76, Texas A&M 55.** One hundred and thirty-one points. The king scored eleven touchdowns and allowed fifty-five and it was still a three-score win. Bawce's postgame filing: "Wear and tear is off." The Record Book has been consulted: it's the **biggest shootout in league history**, past Stew's own 63-57 over Tayne from the Season Two semifinal.
- **Ole Miss 63, Alabama 21.** The champ, rested since Week 4, returned with sixty-three on Odini. "Yall better sit that bum on the bench," Pat suggested — about a real-life quarterback, but the energy carried.
- **LSU 31, Texas 30.** Purp edged Lute on the last possession; Lute's self-scout: "I think I'm the curt cousins of this college football lol. Dinking and dunking all over the field lol."
- **Ohio State 27, Penn State 21** was the Week 11 Game of the Week; States finished the regular season 1-3 in played games, a year removed from 13-0.
- **Oregon 61, USC 7** and **Michigan 56, Nebraska 7** — the two B1G powers warmed up for each other by combining for 117 on the bottom of the conference.
- **Florida State 35, Clemson 28** — Zoe over Lux in Week 12, a preview of the ACC final that never got played.

## Championship Week

**SEC: Oklahoma 24, Ole Miss 14.** Stew-Pat V. The king wins it for the third straight year, hands the champ his only loss of the season, and the belt-race math sharpens: 3-2 Stew with both of them in the field. LT, now the league's video department, cut the hype tape — "Season 48 SEC Championship Stew vs Pat" — and the game lived up to roughly half of it.

**B1G: Oregon 51, Michigan 31.** Bowzer put fifty-one on the vault — the most anyone has scored on Dre in league history — finished 13-0, and takes the first 1-seed of his career. Two years ago he lost this game to States. Last year he lost it to States. This year he hung half a hundred on the best defense in the league. The #big-games desk has a conflict of interest and does not care.

**Big 12: TCU 28, Arizona State 24.** KJ over TBO for the third straight year, by four, in the same game they always play. Three Big 12 crowns in a row. "i should be a tier higher than all of yall" is starting to have evidence.

**ACC: Florida State 38, Clemson 14 — by forfeit.** Zoe's first conference crown, in a rookie season, arrived without a snap: Lux didn't show, and the Seminoles finish 13-0 with a 2-seed. Per league law the crown counts, the game is exempt, and the record book notes the asterisk and moves on. Lux's own review of his comeback year, filed earlier: "I'm genuinely bad pretty sure everyone agrees and is confused how I ever won."

## The Playoff: Glendale or bust

**Seeds, byes to the top four:** 1 Oregon (13-0), 2 Florida State (13-0), 3 Oklahoma (12-1), 4 Ole Miss (12-1), then Michigan, TCU, Ohio State, Arizona State, Nebraska, LSU, Clemson, and CPU qualifier UAB at twelve. Title game: **State Farm Stadium, Glendale.**

**First round:**
- **12 UAB at 5 Michigan** — Dre hosts the CPU; the winner gets the champ in the Cotton Bowl. The vault vs the wrong-QB offense, if the alarm clock holds.
- **9 Nebraska at 8 Arizona State** — the rookie against last year's Cinderella. Roy's two losses were by one and by forty-nine; TBO's were both to KJ. Winner draws unbeaten Oregon in the Rose.
- **11 Clemson at 6 TCU** — Lux's first playoff game since returning, against a three-time Big 12 champion, for the right to meet the king in the Sugar Bowl. Lux's preseason prediction: "I'm saving my luck for playoffs v tbo he's a goat." Wrong Big 12 team, but the luck will be needed.
- **10 LSU at 7 Ohio State** — the Week 1 rematch. Curt won the first one 24-21 in overtime; Purp has scored 51, 34, and 31 since. Winner meets unbeaten Florida State in the Orange. This one's being played as you read this.

**The shape of it:** Oregon and Florida State are on opposite halves — both unbeatens can reach Glendale. **Stew and Pat are on opposite halves too — Stew-Pat VI is only possible in the national championship.** The king's road runs through the TCU/Clemson winner and then the Orange side; the champ's runs through Michigan or UAB and then whoever survives Oregon's quarter. The belt sits 3-2. The committee, for once, seeded the two of them where they can't meet early. The league has never been less sure that's a good thing.
