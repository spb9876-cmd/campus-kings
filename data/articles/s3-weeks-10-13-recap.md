Four weeks, a fifty-point rivalry classic that needed four overtimes, and the loudest story of the stretch run isn't Stew or Pat — it's Alabama waking up right before the bracket gets set. Let's get into it.

---

## The Game Was THE GAME

**Michigan 50, Ohio State 48. Four overtimes.** The Week 13 Game of the Week and the instant game of the year. Curt led by three touchdowns at halftime — at home, in The Shoe, against an unbeaten arch-rival — and Dre erased all twenty-one of it, dragged the thing through four overtimes, and left with his perfect season intact and a Big Ten Championship berth in hand.

Both men knew what they'd just played. "Damn good game bro," from Dre in the game chat. Curt's verdict on himself: "I played like a bitch at the end of regulation." The scoreboard is less harsh — 98 combined points in a rivalry game is not a collapse, it's a war — but the difference between those two sentences is the difference between playing for a conference title this week and watching one.

---

## Jason Finds His Season Just in Time

Three weeks ago Alabama was buried. Then: **Alabama 37, South Carolina 13** in Week 10. **Alabama 49, LSU 7** in Week 11. **Alabama 35, Georgia 14** — the Week 12 Game of the Week — against a team that had just beaten Florida by 21. Three straight, 121 points scored, 34 allowed. If the bubble comes down to who's playing the best football right now, nobody else has the argument Jason has.

---

## Oklahoma Survives, Doesn't Cruise

**Oklahoma 27, Tennessee 21** was the Week 11 Game of the Week, and it was ugly on purpose — a combined FOURTEEN turnovers, per the league broadcast, with the Sooners sealing it late on the fourteenth. Two weeks after the worst loss of his tenure, Stew found a way to win a rock fight instead of losing a track meet. The champ's floor is still higher than everybody's ceiling. His ceiling got tested again in the SEC final — more on that in the Championship Week post.

---

## Penn State Isn't Playing Games

**Penn State 42, Indiana 20** in Week 10, then **Penn State 61, USC 14** in Week 11 — a 103-34 window, and sixty-one is the biggest single-game output in the league this season. States is rolling into the postseason conversation exactly the way he wants to.

---

## Pat Doesn't Blink

Two weeks after hanging 49 on the champ, **Ole Miss 42, LSU 20** closed Pat's regular season. No statement necessary this time — just business, the kind that set up the SEC Championship rematch everybody in the league wanted to see. (You know how that went. Next post.)

## The Rest of the Scoreboard

**Notre Dame 56, USC 21** — Tayne's answer to a choppy month was dropping 56 in the Coliseum. **Texas 35, Texas A&M 14** — Work took the rivalry game comfortably. **TCU 52, Baylor 33** put KJ back on the board, **Clemson 35, Florida State 17** kept LT humming, and **Oregon 35, Indiana 14** pushed Bowzer's quiet march along — quiet, that is, until his own #big-games channel had to cover him in the conference final. The LSU rollercoaster ran its full route: a Week 10 Game of the Week win over Bawce, then 49-7 and 42-20 defeats — this league turns fast. And Cal has a coach again: Lux, four legacy rings in the building, dropped his debut 42-17 to ZBO's SMU. Newcomers start at the bottom. The rings say he won't stay there.
