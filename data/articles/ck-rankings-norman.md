The chair doesn't move. The room did.

**Stew holds No. 1** — that's the rule of this board, and it hasn't changed: two rings against everybody else's one or none, and the top line changes hands in a bracket, not in a Week 3 spotlight game. But let's not pretend Norman didn't happen. A coach in his first full season walked into Oklahoma's building for his Game of the Week debut and won by eighteen. The post-Tampa edition called the gap at the top a coin flip. The coin is still in the air — and now the whole league has seen the king bleed twice in the space of a month.

The résumé math is the résumé math. What the last four weeks changed is everything underneath it.

---

## No. 1

**Stew — Oklahoma.** Two rings. Nobody else has two rings. That sentence has ended every argument on this board since August, and it still does. But the ledger since is getting heavy: swept by Pat, bounced by Bowzer, and now 46-28 in his own stadium to a newcomer making his spotlight debut. His response so far, per the man himself: "haven't turned it on in like 3 days." The bye weeks end. What comes after them decides whether this hold is a pause or a countdown.

## No. 2

**Pat — Ole Miss.** Quietly 2-0 defending the crown — Georgia by eight, BYU by sixteen — while the chat roasts his non-conference slate and Stew publicly roots for him to "lose every game." Champions get that treatment. The gap to No. 1 is unchanged because neither man has played the other; the gap to No. 3 is the one Pat should mind, because the coach below him is playing the best football of the young season.

## No. 3

**Tayne — Notre Dame.** The biggest riser among contenders, and it isn't close. Back-to-back Game of the Week road wins over playoff coaches — 45-35 over top-5 Alabama, 41-27 in Austin over Work — and the #big-games desk has already filed the paperwork: "IRISH STREAKING... a real contender." Three seasons of very-good with no signature run; this is what the signature run looks like when it starts. Strength of schedule does the lifting here — nobody in the league has beaten two better opponents.

## The Bridesmaids

**Bowzer — Oregon**, **LT — Clemson**, and **Dre — Michigan** hold, mostly unplayed against users this stretch — LT's 31-7 handling of the CPU keeps the machine warm, and all three résumés (a one-point Tampa loss, two title-game trips, two B1G crowns) age fine in the garage. **States — Penn State** gets the arrow up off a 42-6 demolition of Indiana in the featured slot — the kind of score that doesn't prove much and still feels like a statement.

## The Climb

**Purp — Tennessee** made the loudest non-Norman noise of the stretch: 35-9 over Curt under the lights at Neyland, one week after Curt hung 58 on UCLA. A Final Four and now a spotlight rout — the résumé is starting to demand top-tier conversation. **JT — Nebraska** makes the biggest single-edition jump in the short history of this board: from the Debut tier to here, on the back of 46-28 over the two-time champ in Norman and a 3-0 career mark against users. One result this loud is a signature; two would be a pattern. **Curt — Ohio State** is the whiplash entry: plus-27 over UCLA, minus-26 at Tennessee, same coach, seven days apart. **Lute — Miami**, **Jason — Alabama**, **KJ — TCU**, and **TBO — Arizona State** hold — TBO's OT survival of LSU in Week 2 still the last thing on his film.

## Proving Ground

**Quan — Florida State** posts the shutout-adjacent line of the year, 47-7 over SMU, and takes a real step out of the pack. **ZBO — SMU** wore that one, then answered with 54 on Cal — a split that somehow raises his floor and lowers his ceiling in the same week. **Work — Texas** and **Ice Man — Georgia** both lost credible games to top-three coaches and hold. **Cell — LSU** is still searching. **Thrash — South Carolina** keeps the Week 1 win over Teeric as the young season's best bottom-tier result.

## The Basement

**Bawce — Texas A&M** gets the arrow up on a 48-28 win over Florida. **Frank — Washington**, **Gooch — Indiana** (42-6 the wrong way), **Bhog — BYU** (gave the champ a half, then gave up 42), **Cozy — Baylor**, **Teeric — USC**, and **Lux — Cal** (54-34 to ZBO in the shootout) are all still hunting the result that moves the needle. It can be done. Ask the guy who just did it in Norman.
