Season Seven is five weeks old and the founder has two Game of the Week wins, a first-ever victory over his own Big 12 nemesis, and a rain-soaked takedown of the runner-up — and still had to listen to Stew tell KJ, "go get my belt." The king is 3-0 and has not been seriously bothered. Everybody else is sorting out who they are.

## Old Man Curt: 2-0, both of them the Game of the Week

Curt billed Week 3 as "Mr Ground and Pound vs Old Man Curt" and put the desk's own headline on it: **#5 Ohio State puts belt to #13 TCU, 34-17** — "Coach King Curt finally gets a win over Coach KJ." That one had history. Hours before kickoff Curt wrote it plainly: "Good? I have never beat you. This is just another game." Then: "That will end tonight. Lmao." It ended. Three interceptions and a forced fumble; Isaiah Ghent 5-of-11 for 164 and a score to Nate Abram. KJ's own scouting report, posted before the game: "good as in i can't win a game outside my conference."

Then Week 5. "Bowzer vs OG Live in a fucking rain storm. 😂" Ohio State 31, Oregon 27, at the Shoe, rain and 58 degrees per the desk. Ghent went 9-of-12 for 216 and three touchdowns with two picks, Marvin Coco caught two balls for two scores, and Dalton Figueroa's 38-yard pick six was the cushion. The Glendale runner-up walked in 4-0 in-game and walked out with his first loss of any kind since the title game. The founder is 4-0 in-game, 2-0 in league play, and both wins were over top-ten coaches. Nobody saw this coming, including, per the usual, the founder.

## The king: 3-0, two Bro Bowls down

Oklahoma opened with the **Battle of the Champions** — the back-to-back king against Jarvis's returning Miami — and won 42-28 behind Taylor Kupper's 299 yards and Santiago Ah You's 7-for-123. Week 2 was the desk's "Battle of the Bros (The Stew Crew)": Stew 30, KJ 28, a game KJ had and didn't keep. Dein Cherry had two interceptions; the desk's verdict was "Coach Stew wins the Bro Bowl." Week 4 was a 50-7 welcome for Quan's Alabama. Three games, two of them against his own crew, zero drama. Stew's one contribution to the offseason debate was a recruiting request: "I need Jarvis Elgin Thompson III in my conference ...."

## The vault, the Rebel, and a Week 1 that mattered

**Dre 35, Pat 21.** The vault opened the year on the two-time champ and took the game, the way it took the Buddy Bowl a year ago. Stew's box score for the night: "Work beat Cobo / Dre beat Patrick." Pat answered in Week 3 with 62 on TBO — the most points anybody has scored on the Cinderella engine — and sits 1-1 with a Week 6 trip to Gainesville already previewed by the desk as the SEC Battle against the rookie at Florida.

## Payback in Tempe, and the Lux Patch

The Week 4 GOTW was billed as **The Rematch**: ASU beat Clemson 31-6 last season. Clemson 31, Arizona State 29. Monte Scales went 10 carries for 131 and two scores and Lux is 2-0 — 21-0 over Jason in Week 1, then the payback. He spent the week before the season on the wrong side of the new rulebook: QB RPOs went on the banned list and Lux took it personally. "Can yall patch stew instead of me Jesus Christ." "Every rule is a lux patch bro I suck." "This lg got soft with all these rules it used to just be don't spam the shit outta one thing lol." LT's ruling stood — "it was on the fence before you" — and KJ, who also ran it, offered the technical defense: "spies and blitzers aren't affected by qb rpos ... Doesn't work on stew bc he blitzes." Lux's closing argument was that he'd been drinking, his kid broke his controller mid-game, and he bowled a 600 series. Then he went out and beat TBO on the road without the play. Patch Stew.
