First board of Season Five, and the full league is on it — all twenty-five active chairs. The Miami Edition ended with the champ on the throne and the belt tied. Five weeks later the throne has a repossession notice on it.

## No. 1 — Stew, Oklahoma (4-0)

The board doesn't move on vibes, so here are the receipts: 26-0 over the 12-0 team, 35-7 over the B1G champion, 26-14 over the Norman thief, 42-41 over a possessed Ice Man. Four games, four of the toughest outs in the league, and the two shutout-adjacent statements came against top-five résumés. The belt is tied at two rings; the season argument is not tied at all. The chair is his until the Sheet says otherwise.

## No. 2 — States, Penn State (3-0)

The only coach alive with a win over the back-to-back champ — 38-28, Week 0, the champ's first title-defense game — plus a Game of the Week dismantling of KJ and a 52-7 formality. Last year the board dinged him for losing to a calendar. This year the calendar is losing. Stew-States is the collision the schedule owes everybody.

## No. 3 — Pat, Ole Miss (0-1)

The two-time defending champion opened the defense with a loss to States and hasn't logged a game since. That's the whole file. Two rings buy a lot of patience — the board remembers who ended last season holding the trophy — but Norman is 4-0 and Happy Valley is 3-0, and the champ's rebuttal window is officially open. He has made this exact climb before, from this exact chair.

## No. 4 — Purp, Tennessee (0-0)

No games, one enormous move: the S4 runner-up bought the rights to LSU while playing out his Tennessee farewell — the first coach in league history to enter a season with a forwarding address. The 127-45 playoff run still headlines the resume. The most dangerous out in the field is now also the most interesting man in the front office.

## No. 5 — Dre, Michigan (1-0)

A 29-10 handling of Georgia, filed without commentary, per policy. The Final Four run holds him here, and his only documented weakness is honesty aversion: "This bot too honest I'm scared to ask what that mf think about me lol." The board, unlike the bot, will say it to his face: top five, no notes.

## No. 6 — KJ, TCU (2-1)

Responded to the States loss the way contenders do: 59-14 and 51-20, a combined 110-34. The 13-0 season didn't end in Miami — it moved into player development. "Sick of living off the portal" is the most ominous sentence a Big 12 opponent can read.

## No. 7 — Tayne, Notre Dame (1-1)
