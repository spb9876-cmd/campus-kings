Four crowns, two unbeatens, one open question that the bracket answered in the cruelest way available: the rubber match might not wait for Miami.

## The crowns

**SEC: Oklahoma 23, Texas A&M 21.** Bawce announced the game his way — "tune in to watch Stew get his ass beat lol" — and then came within a field goal of delivering. The king survived, took his first SEC crown since Season Two, and Bawce's "Good game" afterward read like a man who knows he just played the round of his life and still watched the trophy leave. Two points. That's the whole margin between the No. 1 seed and chaos.

**B1G: Oregon 24, Penn State 14.** One week after States hung 44-21 on Oregon in the regular-season finale, the rematch went the other way — "Oregon vs PSU again," States posted, and again turned out to be the operative word. Bowzer's answer game gave him the conference title and the 5-seed; States still banked the 4-seed and a bye, which is the quietest good outcome of championship weekend.

**Big 12: TCU 45, Arizona State 14.** The most emphatic result of the weekend. KJ went back-to-back on Big 12 crowns, finished the run 13-0, and put 45 on the same TBO team he beat in the Week 8 Game of the Week. And for all of that, the committee handed him... the 9-seed. More on that crime below.

**ACC: Florida State over Clemson, on a forfeit.** The crown counts — that's the league rule, same as it ever was — and it makes Quan a conference champion in his first full season with the new team. The game itself is exempt from every record, and Clemson still drew the 3-seed and a bye. It's the second straight season LT's playoff path has a forfeit in it. Last year it knocked him out of a 13-0 season; this year it cost him a trophy without costing him position.

## The field: twelve teams, one collision course

The full bracket, first round on the left:

- **12 New Mexico at 5 Oregon** — the CPU auto-qualifier (12-1, ranked 19th) draws Bowzer, with 4-seed Penn State waiting in the Rose Bowl quarterfinal. Last season's UAB role, recast.
- **9 TCU at 8 Ole Miss** — an unbeaten, 13-0, back-to-back conference champion, seeded ninth and sent on the road... to the defending national champion, at 11-1. This is the round-one game of the decade, and whoever survives it gets **No. 1 Oklahoma in the Cotton Bowl**.
- **11 Florida State at 6 Michigan** — Quan's ACC champs at Dre's Michigan, winner to face 3-seed Clemson in the Fiesta.
- **10 Nebraska at 7 Tennessee** — JT's first career playoff game, in Knoxville, against the coach who turned Neyland into a fortress this season. The winner gets unbeaten Notre Dame in the Peach.

Read that TCU-Ole Miss line again, because the bracket buried its lede: if the champ survives the unbeaten Frogs, **Stew-Pat III arrives in the quarterfinal**. The series is one apiece across two seasons — Pat's sweep and ring last year, Stew's 38-20 answer in Week 11 — and the committee just scheduled the rubber match two rounds before anyone wanted it. One of the two best coaches in the league is not making the Final Four.

## The stakes, restated

Tayne's Notre Dame is the only bye team without a ring case on the line — his 12-0 is the cleanest résumé in the field and the CK Rankings board has him one thin line behind the champ. LT gets a third crack at the bracket that has ended him twice in title games. States and Bowzer split their two meetings a week apart and could see each other a third time in the semis. And the belt math looms over all of it: Stew wins it all and the race goes 3-1 with the argument basically over; Pat repeats and it's 2-2 at the halfway-to-halfway mark; anybody else wins and the belt conversation gains a third voice.

One more storm cloud, courtesy of the champ himself: Pat posted his coach-management screen — OC Mike Norvell and DC Pete Golding, both A+ rated, both with one year remaining — captioned "Was fun while it lasted." The staff-poaching era is coming for Oxford right as the defense of the crown gets serious.

Bowl Week 1 chats are made. The bracket is live on the site as results land. Miami is waiting.
